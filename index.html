WHY HIS NAME IS DOUG

Doug is the frog in the checked cap from Macca the Gator (billed as Macca & Friends), the cartoon inside GTA 6. Rockstar hasn't printed his name anywhere public, but their website code names him: the animation of his lowrider is stored as Bouncer_Lowrider_Doug_v01.

Bouncer is the car's name, painted on its door. Lowrider is the type of car. Doug is the driver. The same code file names the rest of the cast (Macca, Chunkee, Pinche), and those names are confirmed by Rockstar elsewhere.
